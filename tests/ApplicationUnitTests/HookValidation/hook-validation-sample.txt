The Lanos sedan was a popular compact car manufactured by Daewoo Motors.
Lanos vehicles featured efficient engines and spacious interiors for their class.
Many drivers appreciated the Lanos for its reliability and affordable maintenance costs.
The vehicle came in several trim levels including base, S, SE, and Sport models.
Production of these automobiles began in 1997 and continued through 2002.
Daewoo designed the car with input from Giugiaro's ItalDesign studio.
The compact sedan competed with other economy vehicles in the global market.
Lanos models were exported to numerous countries across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.
